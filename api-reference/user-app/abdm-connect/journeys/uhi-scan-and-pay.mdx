## Who and when

A patient standing at the billing or registration counter of a hospital, clinic or lab. They have a bill to settle: a consultation, a test, a procedure. The counter shows a QR code for the facility. Rather than queue to pay, the patient scans the QR with a Personal Health Record (PHR) app built on Eka, and pays from their phone.

**Already in place:** your app is a PHR app. The patient has an ABHA and is logged in on your app, so you hold Eka's id for the patient ([Log in to an existing ABHA](/api-reference/user-app/abdm-connect/workflows/login-abha)). The facility is a Health Information Provider (HIP) on ABDM and displays a Scan and Pay QR at the counter.

## What they want

"Let me pay this bill from my phone without waiting in line, and give me a receipt."

## The steps

1. The patient scans the QR code at the counter with your app. The QR carries the facility's HIP ID and the counter ID.
2. Your app shares the patient's ABHA profile with the facility and asks for the patient's open orders. → workflow: [Pay an open order with Scan and Pay](/api-reference/user-app/abdm-connect/workflows/scan-and-pay)
3. The facility returns the unpaid orders for this patient, grouped by category with each service and its amount. Your app shows them. → same workflow
4. The patient selects what they want to pay for. Your app sends the selection and receives a payment link with the amount due. → same workflow
5. The patient pays on the payment link. This is a human step on the payment page.
6. Your app fetches the transaction details and shows the payment status and a receipt link. → same workflow

## What they have at the end

The selected bill is paid. The patient holds a receipt link and a transaction id for the payment. The transaction details tie the payment to the patient's ABHA address and the facility's order number.

## Branches

- **The patient has no open order at this facility.** The open order list comes back empty, and the patient pays at the counter as usual. → same workflow, open order step
- **The facility does not answer, or answers with an error.** The request ends in an error, with an error code and message. → same workflow, "When it fails"
- **The payment fails or the patient abandons the payment page.** The transaction details show a status other than paid. {/* TODO: confirm: can the patient retry with the same request, or must they scan again? */} → same workflow, transaction details step
- **The patient is not logged in on your app.** Log them in first. → workflow: [Log in to an existing ABHA](/api-reference/user-app/abdm-connect/workflows/login-abha)
