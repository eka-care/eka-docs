## How they fit together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find or register the patient">
    Look up the patient in the [patient directory](/docs/emr/patients), or create a profile for a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the consultation">
    Start an EkaScribe session and upload the audio, or [stream it over a WebSocket](/docs/scribe/sessions/stream-audio). The [quickstart](/docs/scribe/quickstart) goes from audio to a note in five calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the structured note">
    The note arrives in your template and as [FHIR](/docs/scribe/resources/fhir-structure) (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources). Create the [prescription](/docs/emr/prescription) from its medications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Parse the lab report">
    Send the report to [document parsing](/docs/models/medical-document-parsing). You get each test with its measured value and unit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it to the record">
    [Upload the record](/docs/health-records/upload-records) so the patient and their next doctor can see the visit.
  </Step>
</Steps>
