You build what the patient sees. A clinic's website books appointments into its Eka EMR. A patient-facing agent answers questions, guides the patient to the right care and books the visit. The patient's reports and records are stored and shared from one place. Five Eka products sit behind these.

## What you build with

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Agents" icon="robot" href="/docs/products/agents">
    Build the patient's agent with the Echo Agent Kit, or embed MedAssist as it is.
  </Card>
  <Card title="EMR" icon="calendar-check" href="/docs/emr/appointments">
    Finds open slots, books the appointment and sends reminders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Billing" icon="credit-card" href="/docs/billing/payments">
    Records the payment for the consultation, with a receipt ID.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Health Records" icon="folder-open" href="/docs/health-records/read-documents">
    Lists and retrieves the patient's documents and records.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Models" icon="clipboard-check" href="/docs/get-started/whats-possible/health-assessment">
    Runs an AI health quiz in your app, built with four function calls.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How they fit together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the conversation">
    Embed [MedAssist](/docs/agents/medassist) in your app, or build an agent with the [Echo Agent Kit quickstart](/docs/agents/agent-kit/quickstart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book the visit">
    The agent books a slot through [appointments](/docs/emr/appointments). The [appointments recipe](/docs/get-started/whats-possible/appointment-management) builds the booking screen end to end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the payment">
    Record the [payment](/docs/billing/payments) for the consultation, in full or in parts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remind and follow up">
    Send the reminder with the [notify API](/api-sdk/emr/notify/notify) before the visit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share reports and assessments">
    Show the patient's [records](/docs/health-records/read-documents), and run a [health quiz](/docs/get-started/whats-possible/health-assessment) between visits.
  </Step>
</Steps>
