You build your own clinical tools on Eka's APIs, MCP servers and SDKs. Four products cover the work, from the consultation to the knowledge your AI checks against. Each one has its own documentation, API reference and changelog.

## What you build with

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Medical Scribe" icon="microphone" href="/docs/scribe/overview">
    Consultations become structured notes that complete the chart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Medical Document Parsing" icon="brain" href="/docs/models/medical-document-parsing">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Medical Agents" icon="robot" href="/docs/products/agents">

  </Card>

## How they fit together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take the report in">
    Upload a lab report to [document parsing](/docs/models/medical-document-parsing). A [webhook](/docs/models/medical-document-parsing/smart-report-webhook) tells you when the result is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Code the clinical terms">
    Send diagnoses and findings to [entity codification](/docs/models/medical-entity-codification). You get codes such as ICD-10, SNOMED CT and LOINC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check against medical knowledge">
    Look up drugs, labs and protocols in the [medical database](/docs/datasets/medical-db) and the [knowledge bases](/docs/datasets/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your AI the same tools">
    Connect an assistant to the [MedAI Tools MCP server](/docs/datasets/build-with-ai/mcp-server/introduction), so its answers rest on verified data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
