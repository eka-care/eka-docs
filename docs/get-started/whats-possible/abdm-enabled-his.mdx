This recipe takes a hospital system from the front desk to the insurance claim on India's health stack. It uses [ABDM Connect](/docs/hie-cm/about) and [NHCX](/docs/nhcx/getting-started). Start with the [ABDM Connect quickstart](/docs/hie-cm/quickstart) to get your keys and your first call working.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the patient with ABHA">
    Create or verify the patient's ABHA at registration with [Milestone 1](/docs/hie-cm/flows/m1). At a busy counter, register patients from a QR code with [scan and share](/docs/hie-cm/scan-and-share/getting-started).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link each visit to the ABHA">
    After the visit, link its records to the patient's ABHA as a provider with [Milestone 2](/docs/hie-cm/flows/m2). The [register and link journey](/docs/hie-cm/journeys/hip-register-patient-and-link-visit) shows the whole flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch records from other hospitals with consent">
    Ask for the patient's records from other providers and receive them once the patient consents, with [Milestone 3](/docs/hie-cm/flows/m3). See the [fetch with consent journey](/docs/hie-cm/journeys/hiu-fetch-records-with-consent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your doctors and facility">
    Enrol your doctors and your facility in the national registries with [Milestone 4](/docs/hie-cm/flows/m4).
  </Step>
  <Step title="File the insurance claim">
    Check eligibility, request pre-authorisation and submit the claim through [NHCX](/docs/nhcx/getting-started).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To have a coding agent build this with you, see [Build with AI for ABDM Connect](/docs/hie-cm/build-with-ai).
