## ABDM Connect

**Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)** is India's national digital health infrastructure. It creates a shared ecosystem where patients, hospitals, labs, insurers, and health apps can exchange health data securely, with patient consent driving every transaction.

Eka's **ABDM Connect** is a plug-and-play API and SDK suite that handles the ABDM gateway complexity for you, so your team can focus on the product rather than the plumbing.

## The ABDM Ecosystem

### Core Registries

ABDM is built on four registries. Every entity gets a unique, verifiable identity through one of these.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="ABHA" icon="id-card">
    **Ayushman Bharat Health Account**

    The patient's health identity. Can be a 14-digit **ABHA Number** (KYC-verified via Aadhaar) or an **ABHA Address** like `nisha@abdm`. All health transactions in ABDM are anchored to this.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HPR" icon="user-doctor">
    **Healthcare Professionals Registry**

    A national registry of licensed healthcare professionals. Each doctor, nurse, or practitioner gets a unique **HPR ID** for verified digital identity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HFR" icon="hospital">
    **Health Facility Registry**

    A national registry of health facilities, both government and private. Each facility gets a unique **HFR ID** used for record linking and patient discovery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consent Manager" icon="shield-halved">
    **Consent Manager**

    Manages the consent flow between HIPs (who hold data) and HIUs (who want access). The ABDM Gateway itself acts as the Consent Manager.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Roles in the Ecosystem

There are two roles in ABDM. A product can play both.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="HIP" icon="database">
    **Health Information Provider**

    Hospitals, labs, clinics, and pharmacies that generate and hold patient health records. After a visit or test, a HIP links the records to the patient's ABHA address so they're discoverable and shareable.

    *Examples: hospital HMS, diagnostic lab system, pharmacy software*
  </Card>
  <Card title="HIU" icon="magnifying-glass-chart">
    **Health Information User**

    Any entity that requests access to a patient's health records with the patient's consent. After consent is granted, the HIU receives the records from the relevant HIPs.

    *Examples: insurance company, specialist referral system, wellness platform*
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## CARE: the four milestones

ABDM certifies hospital and lab software (HMIS/LMIS) in four milestones. Read them as CARE: **C**reate, **A**ttach, **R**etrieve, **E**nrol. Eka's ABDM Connect covers all four.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create (M1): the patient's ABHA">
    Needed by every integration.

    Before any record can be linked or shared, the patient needs an ABHA. Your product lets patients create an ABHA or log in with an existing one, view and update their profile, download their ABHA card, and check in at your facility by scanning a QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach (M2): records to the ABHA">
    Needed by hospitals, labs, clinics, and pharmacies (HIPs).

    After each visit or test, the records are linked to the patient's ABHA address. Patients can also find records you hold and link them themselves. When the patient consents to share them, your records are sent to whoever asked. Eka can store the records and handle sharing for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retrieve (M3): records, with consent">
    Needed by anyone who wants to view a patient's records (HIUs).

    You ask the patient for consent, the patient approves or denies it in their app, and once approved you receive the records from every provider they chose. Patients can revoke consent at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrol (M4): doctors and facilities">
    Needed by every hospital and lab software integration.

    Doctors and nurses get an HPR ID and facilities get an HFR ID, so they can take part in digital health transactions. Eka also links your facilities to Eka's bridge so records can flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Journeys

One patient, all of CARE. Meera walks into a clinic without an ABHA; the receptionist creates one with her Aadhaar OTP (Create) and attaches the visit to it (Attach). A month later her insurer asks for the records. She approves the request in her PHR app, and the insurer retrieves the FHIR bundle from the clinic (Retrieve).

None of this works until the clinic and its doctors are enrolled in HFR and HPR (Enrol). The same story told from Meera's own app is the PHR track.

## PHR App

A PHR (Personal Health Record) app is a separate ABDM track, distinct from the milestones above. It is the patient-facing side of ABDM: patients create their ABHA, see records from every provider they've visited, manage consent, and share their profile at health facilities.

A PHR app typically acts as both a HIP (storing records the patient uploads) and a HIU (fetching records from other providers on the patient's behalf). It covers:

- ABHA creation and patient login
- A single view of every linked record
- Approving or denying consent requests
- **Scan & Share**: the patient scans a QR code at a facility to share their profile for registration
- **Health locker**: storage the patient controls for their own documents
- **Notifications** when a provider links a new record

## What You Can Build

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Hospital / Clinic Product (HMS)" icon="hospital-user">
    If you run a hospital or clinic system, you need to be a HIP. After every visit or test, link the patient's records to their ABHA address. When a patient later consents to share them with another entity (like an insurer), your system serves those records automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consumer Health App (PHR)" icon="heart-pulse">
    If you're building a patient-facing app, you need the PHR track on top of the milestones. Patients can bring together their entire health history from every facility they've visited, manage consent, and check in at facilities by scanning a QR code.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Insurance / Analytics Platform (HIU)" icon="file-waveform">
    If you need to access a patient's health records from any ABDM-connected provider, you need to be an HIU. You request consent from the patient and, once it is granted, receive their records directly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrated Health Platform" icon="layer-group">
    Large hospital networks and health platforms often need to be HIP, HIU, and PHR at once. Your HMS links records (HIP), doctors can pull history from outside (HIU), and patients have a portal to manage everything (PHR).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
