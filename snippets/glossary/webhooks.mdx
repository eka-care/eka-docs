| Term | What it means | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Webhook | An HTTP callback Eka sends to your endpoint when something happens, so you do not poll. The payload carries the resource ID and the action, and you call the API for full details. | [Webhooks](/api-sdk/webhooks/getting-started) |
| Webhook signature | The `Eka-Webhook-Signature` header Eka adds when you give a signing key at registration. You verify it to confirm the event came from Eka unchanged. | [Webhook signature](/docs/webhooks/webhook-signature) |
