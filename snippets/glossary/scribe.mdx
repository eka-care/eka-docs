| Term | What it means | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| EkaScribe | Eka's AI medical scribe. You record or stream a consultation or dictation and get back a transcript and structured clinical notes in the format you define. | [EkaScribe overview](/docs/scribe/overview) |
| EkaScribe session | One unit of work in EkaScribe, identified by a `session_id`. It ties audio capture, processing and results together: you create it, send audio, end it, then read the notes. | [Sessions overview](/api-sdk/scribe/sessions/overview) |
| Template | A note format you describe in plain markdown, such as a SOAP note or a discharge summary. You name templates when you create a session, and EkaScribe returns one structured note per template. | [Create template](/api-sdk/scribe/templates/create-template) |
| `pro` and `lite` | The two EkaScribe models. `pro` adds speaker diarization, custom templates and 60-minute sessions; `lite` is fast and built for dictation. | [EkaScribe overview](/docs/scribe/overview) |
