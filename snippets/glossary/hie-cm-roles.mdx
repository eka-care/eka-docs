| Term | What it means | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| ABDM | Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, India's national digital health infrastructure. Patients, hospitals, labs, insurers and health apps exchange health data through it, with the patient's consent driving every exchange. | [ABDM overview](/docs/hie-cm/about) |
| ABDM Connect | Eka's REST API over the ABDM gateway. You call Eka, and Eka handles ABDM's gateway protocol, encryption and callbacks for you. | [ABDM Connect overview](/api-sdk/hie-cm/overview) |
| CARE | How these docs name the four ABDM milestones: Create (M1, the patient's ABHA), Attach (M2, records to the ABHA), Retrieve (M3, records with consent), Enrol (M4, doctors and facilities in HPR and HFR). | [What is ABDM Connect](/docs/hie-cm/about#care-the-four-milestones) |
| HIE-CM | The ABDM family that covers ABHA, care contexts, consent and records. Its specification also sets the ECDH scheme used to encrypt records in transit. | [Encryption](/docs/hie-cm/care-contexts/ecdh-encryption) |
| Consent Manager | The ABDM registry that manages consent between the parties that hold data and the parties that want it. The ABDM gateway itself acts as the Consent Manager. | [ABDM overview](/docs/hie-cm/about#core-registries) |
| HIP | Health Information Provider. A hospital, lab, clinic or pharmacy that holds patient records and links them to the patient's ABHA address so they can be found and shared. | [Link a care context as a HIP](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/link-care-context-hip) |
| HIU | Health Information User. Any party, such as an insurer or referral system, that requests a patient's records with consent and receives them from the HIPs. | [Request a patient's consent as a HIU](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/hiu-request-consent) |
| PHR app | A personal health record app, the patient-facing side of ABDM. It usually acts as both a HIP for records the patient uploads and a HIU for records it fetches. | [Gather every health record in a PHR app](/docs/hie-cm/journeys/phr-patient-aggregates-records) |
| Milestone | One of the four stages, M1 to M4, in which ABDM certifies hospital and lab software. They cover identity, linking, consent and registries. | [ABDM overview](/docs/hie-cm/about#abdm-milestones) |
| HPR | Healthcare Professionals Registry. Each doctor, nurse or practitioner on it gets a unique HPR ID as a verified digital identity. | [Create an HPR ID with Aadhaar OTP](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/create-hpr-id) |
| HFR | Health Facility Registry. Each government or private facility on it gets a unique HFR ID, used for record linking and patient discovery. | [Register and onboard a facility](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/register-hfr) |
