| Term | What it means | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| NHCX | National Health Claims Exchange. It carries cashless insurance claims, including PMJAY, between your hospital and the payer. ABDM Connect builds, signs and encrypts the FHIR bundles for you. | [NHCX overview](/docs/nhcx/getting-started) |
| Claim | The NHCX case that every call after setup hangs off. You create one per hospitalisation and keep its `claim_id` against your admission. | [NHCX overview](/docs/nhcx/getting-started) |
| Pre-auth | The NHCX request for approval you submit with the treating doctor, diagnosis, documents and answers. The payer rejects a pre-auth above the policy's wallet balance. | [NHCX overview](/docs/nhcx/getting-started) |
