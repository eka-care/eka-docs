| Term | What it means | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Care context | A visit, test or admission at your facility, linked to the patient's ABHA address. Once linked, it is registered with ABDM, and any HIU the patient consents to can request it. | [Link a care context as a HIP](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/link-care-context-hip) |
| Linking | Registering a care context under the patient's ABHA. You link new records yourself; the patient links older records through discovery. Linking returns `202 Accepted` and confirms on the `abha.link_care_context` webhook. | [Link a care context as a HIP](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/link-care-context-hip) |
| Discovery | A patient searches your facility from a PHR app for records that were never linked to their ABHA. You answer three webhooks: return the matches, send an OTP, then confirm the link. | [Answer discovery and link old records](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/hip-discovery-and-link) |
| FHIR bundle | The record format a HIP sends for a care context. If Eka stores your data, you send it base64-encoded in the `data` field when you link. Otherwise you build and encrypt it on each fetch request. | [Serve records on a fetch request](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/serve-data-on-fetch) |
| HI type | Health information type, the kind of record in a care context, for example `OPConsultation` or `Prescription`. You send it as `hi_type` when you link, and a fetch request lists the `hi_types` the HIU wants. | [Link a care context as a HIP](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/link-care-context-hip) |
| Consent request | A HIU's request for a patient's records. It appears in the patient's PHR app, where they approve or deny it, and its status changes arrive on the `abha.consent_update` webhook. | [Request a patient's consent as a HIU](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/hiu-request-consent) |
| Consent artefact | The record ABDM generates when a patient approves a consent request, one per HIP. An expired artefact returns `ABDM-1061`, and you raise a new consent. | [Approve, deny or revoke a consent in a PHR app](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/phr-act-on-consent) |
| Consent PIN | The PIN a patient sets in a PHR app and verifies when they approve, deny or revoke a consent. | [Approve, deny or revoke a consent in a PHR app](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/phr-act-on-consent) |
| Auto-approval | A policy that lets the patient authorise a class of consent requests once, instead of approving each one. | [Set up the patient's health locker](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/set-up-health-locker) |
| Health locker | Storage the patient controls for their own records. When auto-approval is on, your PHR app is the locker, and Eka tells it each time a HIP links a new record. | [Set up the patient's health locker](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/set-up-health-locker) |
| Subscription | The standing arrangement Eka sets up with auto-approval. It sends `abha.subscription_notify` when a provider links a new record for the patient, and `abha.subscription_modified` when it changes. | [Set up the patient's health locker](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/set-up-health-locker) |
| ECDH | The encryption scheme for records sent between HIPs and HIUs: ECDH key agreement on Curve25519, with HKDF-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM. If Eka stores your records, Eka encrypts and decrypts them for you. | [Encryption](/docs/hie-cm/care-contexts/ecdh-encryption) |
