| Term | What it means | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| ABHA | Ayushman Bharat Health Account, the patient's health identity. Every health transaction in ABDM is anchored to it. It takes the form of an ABHA number or an ABHA address. | [ABDM overview](/docs/hie-cm/about#core-registries) |
| ABHA number | The 14-digit, KYC-verified form of an ABHA. You create one from an Aadhaar OTP, with Aadhaar as the KYC. | [Create an ABHA with Aadhaar OTP](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/create-abha-aadhaar-otp) |
| ABHA address | The patient's ABHA handle, such as `shyam@abdm`. A HIP links records to it. You can create one without KYC from a mobile OTP. | [Create an ABHA address with mobile OTP](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/create-abha-mobile-otp) |
| ABHA Gateway session | The patient's session with the ABHA Gateway, separate from your client token. Creating or registering an ABHA opens it, and patient-scoped calls without a live session return HTTP `491`. | [Hold the ABHA Gateway session](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/hold-gateway-session) |
| Scan & Share | The patient scans your facility's QR code with a PHR app to share their ABHA profile. Eka sends you the profile on a webhook, and you reply with an OPD token. | [Receive a scanned profile as a HIP](/docs/hie-cm/workflows/hip-scan-and-share) |
