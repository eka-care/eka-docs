Everything from Eka MedAI that isn't a [model](/eka-medai/models) or a [dataset](/eka-medai/datasets): evaluation tooling, knowledge graphs, document tooling, knowledge bases served over API and MCP, and the reference lists behind our services. For SDKs and agent frameworks across all Eka products, see the [Open Source](/user-guides/open-source) page.

## Evaluation

- **KARMA – OpenMedEvalKit** – Open-source framework for evaluating medical AI models on Indian clinical tasks; runs every dataset on the [Datasets](/eka-medai/datasets) page. [GitHub](https://github.com/eka-care/karMA-OpenMedEvalKit) · [Launch blog](https://info.eka.care/services/introducing-karma-openmedevalkit-an-open-source-framework-for-medical-ai-evaluation)

## Knowledge Graphs

- **BODHI – Bharat Ontology for Disease & Healthcare Informatics** – Clinician-validated clinical knowledge graphs (conditions ↔ symptoms ↔ specialities; concepts ↔ drugs ↔ lab investigations) in Neo4j, CSV, PyTorch Geometric and RDF. [GitHub](https://github.com/eka-care/BODHI) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ekacare/BODHI-S) · [Launch blog](https://info.eka.care/services/bodhi-bharat-ontology-for-disease-healthcare-informatics)

## Document Tooling

- **document-pii-redactor** – Detects and redacts PII in text and document images; CPU-friendly, trained for Indian names, documents and languages. [GitHub](https://github.com/eka-care/document-pii-redactor) · [Model weights](https://huggingface.co/ekacare/document-pii-redactor) · [Live demo](https://medai.eka.care/pii-redactor)
- **Med Doc Classifier** – Lightweight medical document classifier. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/ekacare/med-doc-classifier) · [Live demo](https://medai.eka.care/classifier)

## Knowledge Bases (API & MCP)

Curated Indian medical knowledge, available as web APIs and through the [MedAI Tools MCP Server](/ai-tools/medai-tools-mcp/introduction) for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other MCP clients. The same knowledge bases are also exposed inside the [Eka EMR MCP Server](/ai-tools/mcp-server/introduction).

| Knowledge base | What it covers | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Indian Branded Drugs | 500,000+ Indian branded drugs with generic composition, form, therapy class and manufacturer | [API](/api-reference/knowledge-base/medications/search) · [MCP](/ai-tools/medai-tools-mcp/tools) |
| Pharmacology (NFI) | National Formulary of India: indications, dosing, contraindications, pregnancy safety | [API](/api-reference/knowledge-base/medications/pharmacology) · [MCP](/ai-tools/medai-tools-mcp/tools) |
| Indian Treatment Protocols | 180+ protocols from ICMR, RSSDI, ADA, WHO, KDIGO, AHA and others, for RAG grounding | [API](/api-reference/knowledge-base/protocols/search) · [MCP](/ai-tools/medai-tools-mcp/tools) |
| Medical Calculators | 400+ clinical calculators across 26 categories | [MCP](/ai-tools/medai-tools-mcp/tools) |
| Medical Entity Codification | SNOMED CT, LOINC, ICD-10-CM and medication linking for free-text entities | [API](/api-reference/health-ai/medical-entity-codification/overview) · [Guide](/eka-medai/technologies/codification/overview) |
