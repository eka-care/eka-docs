  - **Kumar et al. (2025)** – [ASR Under the Stethoscope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10967) · [Eka Medical ASR dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ekacare/eka-medical-asr-evaluation-dataset)
